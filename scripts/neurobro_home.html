Financial markets are complex.
We build AI that makes them simple, and lets you act.

Most tools analyze a market and then send you somewhere else to trade.
Neurobro keeps both in one app on one account,
so you can go from a question to a filled order without giving up custody of your coins.

Neurobro is the intelligence layer for finance.
We store and analyze market data.
We build specialized AI agents to simplify this data.
And we ship applications where you can research any market
and trade it from a wallet only you control.

// NEUROWALLET

Trade from a wallet only you control

NeuroWallet is built into Neurobro on web and mobile. Ask about a market, then buy or sell it in the same app, without handing over your keys.

Non-custodial by design

Your keys are generated and secured in a hardware-backed environment. Neurobro never takes custody and cannot move your funds.

Base and Solana, more coming

Buy and sell across Base and Solana, with cross-chain routing handled for you.

Gas sponsored

We cover network fees on supported routes, and trading fees start at 0.30%.
