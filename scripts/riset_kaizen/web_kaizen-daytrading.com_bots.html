The problem with most bots

Most trading bots only work in one kind of market and give back their gains in the wrong regime. A trend bot bleeds in chop; a mean-reversion bot gets run over in a strong trend. The environment changes — a single fixed strategy does not.

How it works

1

The engine classifies the day

Every day before the open, the regime engine classifies the session using five inputs: VIX level, VIX vs. its 200-day trend, ATR ratio, the Trade Policy Uncertainty index, and realized volatility.

2

Your bot loads it automatically

Your bot pulls the daily classification on its own — no manual updates, no CSV maintenance. If the download ever fails, it stays flat as a fail-safe.

3

Exactly one approach runs — or none

Just one of four specialized approaches is active (mean-reversion at zones, absorption order flow, TPO continuation, delta-zone fades) — or the bot stays OFF on days with no historical edge (~17% of days).

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to configure anything? +

No. Enter your license key, choose the number of contracts (ES or MES), and enable trading. The regime engine steers all strategy parameters automatically each day.

How does the bot know which strategy runs today? +

It pulls the regime classification from our server once a day, automatically. If the download fails, it stays flat as a fail-safe.

Does the bot trade every day? +

No — on days with no historically demonstrable edge it deliberately stays flat (~17% of days in the backtest).

Which markets? +

ES / MES (E-mini and Micro S&P 500). More markets may follow later.

Does it work with my prop firm? +

It is built around Apex/Bulenox rules (trailing-drawdown-aware MES sizing). You remain responsible for your firm’s rules.

Can I see the code or set my own parameters? +

No, the strategy logic is protected. You can set: contracts, ES/MES, and on/off.

What happens if I cancel? +

Access continues until the end of the paid month, after which the software deactivates automatically.

Do I need a VPS? +

Recommended if your PC does not reliably run during trading hours (e.g. ChartVPS, ~$40–60/month) — optional, not a requirement.
