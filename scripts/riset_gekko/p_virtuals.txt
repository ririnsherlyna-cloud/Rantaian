Self-Improving and Resilient Agents Developers crafting AI agents via the G.A.M.E framework will be able to utilize intelligence from the Allora Network to unlock a wide range of new onchain use cases, such as: Automated Looping Strategy Agents: Agents can utilize Allora’s price and upcoming volatility forecasts to autonomously adjust leverage of leveraged looping strategies by increasing exposure during favorable conditions and reducing it during downturns. This approach minimizes liquidation risks, automates profit-taking, and creates safer and more efficient looping strategies. Trading Strategy Agents: With precise price and volatility forecasts, developers can have their agents execute bespoke trading strategies, whether directional or delta-neutral. These strategies incorporate advanced risk management techniques, allowing users to explore more refined trading methods. Meta-Strategy and Intelligent Dynamic Strategy Agents: Allora’s intelligence network will soon be able to provide forecasts for metrics such as liquidity, trading volume, and interest rates, enabling agents to proactively optimize yield strategies. Meta-strategies further enhance efficiency by reallocating capital across sub-strategies expected to deliver the highest returns. Intelligent DCA Strategies: Agents leverage multi-interval forecasts to dynamically time and size asset purchases, outperforming traditional dollar-cost averaging methods. By purchasing at forecasted optimal moments, these strategies maximize user asset acquisition over time. Prediction Market Agents: Using aggregated insights from Allora’s flexible intelligence network, agents can accurately trade on prediction markets for events like elections, sports, and financial outcomes. This precision enables them to generate returns by trading market volatility and capitalizing on undervalued opportunities. With these capabilities, AI agents can now leverage: ‌ Adaptive Intelligence : AI agents become capable of dynamically adjusting their behavior and improving over time. ‌ Optimized Performance : By incorporating Allora’s predictive insights, agents are able to proactively take action on changes in a market before they occur to maximize capital efficiency. ‌ Resilience in Complex Scenarios : Agents benefit from a diverse intelligence network, enabling them to perform onchain strategies with heightened accuracy and reliability.
