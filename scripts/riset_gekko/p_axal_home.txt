 Axal: High Yield Savings # Axal: High Yield Savings Axal: High Yield Savings is a wealth management app from Lockbox Technologies, Inc. (doing business as Axal). Put dollars, Bitcoin, and gold to work with up to 7.6% APY on cash, the lowest Bitcoin fees, and insured, physical gold bars. No minimums, no lockups, and no middlemen. Dollar deposits earn real-time, auto-compounding yield allocated to tier-1 overcollateralized DeFi lending markets. Axal monitors risk and rebalances so you keep industry-leading APY without lockups. Withdraw whenever you want. Traditional banks lend out deposits and keep the spread; Axal passes interest back to you every second. Bitcoin on Axal is built to be the cheapest place to buy and hold. We pass through market quotes from decentralized exchanges with no extra spread from Axal, which is less than half the fee of leading centralized exchanges. You can also buy physical gold bars through Axal and hold gold alongside cash and Bitcoin in one account. Get started in a few minutes with three steps: create an account with just an email, fund from Apple Pay, Google Pay, a card, a bank, or another wallet, and start earning. No prior experience is required. Calliemarie616 FAQs What is Axal, and how is it different from my bank/traditional investment accounts? ⌄ Axal is a high yield savings app for dollars, Bitcoin, and gold that provides industry leading rates and protection. Traditional banks lend out your money and pocket the earnings - Axal passes interest straight back to you in real time, with no middlemen, lockups or hidden fees. How do I get started with the app? ⌄ Download the mobile app on the App Store or Google Play. You can also use the web app by clicking Launch App at the top of this page. How is the yield (APY) generated? ⌄ Your funds are automatically allocated to tier-1, overcollateralized DeFi lending platforms. Axal continuously monitors and rebalances your portfolio to maintain APY targets and minimize risk. This is similar to what banks do with your money, but with code to enforce collateral rules and liquidations for efficiency and stability. Can I use Axal if I’m new to crypto or don’t have a wallet? ⌄ Absolutely. You can purchase assets directly through Axal, which supports debit cards and bank transfers. No prior crypto experience or separate wallet required. If you already own crypto, you can also transfer your existing USDC or Bitcoin to your Axal wallet. What fees are associated with using the app? ⌄ Axal currently has 0 fees, charging users market quotes for Bitcoin and gold trades. This is less than half of the fees of leading centralized exchanges. Axal has no fees on yield generated and no fee on wallet-to-wallet transfers.
