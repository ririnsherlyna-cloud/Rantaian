All Collections Trading Bots Signal Bot Tutorials How Keep My Position Open Works How Keep My Position Open Works A short explanation on how the Keep My Position Open order functions on 3Commas. August 11, 2026 When you create a strategy on the spot market without setting a Take Profit or Stop Loss, the trade will be marked as "Completed" immediately—provided the order type is market and not limit. This behavior is due to the nature of the spot market, where there is no concept of an "open position", as you are purchasing real assets that are stored in your wallet. However, when Keep My Position Open (Spot exchange only) is enabled, the system will continue monitoring the strategy on the spot market even if the Take Profit or Stop Loss are not set. As a result, the strategy will not be marked as "Completed" immediately after the opening.
